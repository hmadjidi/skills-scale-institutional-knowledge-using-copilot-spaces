# OctoAcme Project Management Processes

OctoAcme manages projects through a lifecycle that starts with **initiation**: validating the business need, aligning stakeholders, and deciding whether to proceed. **Planning** turns approved work into a prioritized backlog with estimates, milestones, acceptance criteria, and identified risks and dependencies. During **execution**, teams track work on a project board, follow pull request and testing practices, review progress through regular team rhythms, and escalate blockers. Risk management and stakeholder communication continue throughout delivery. **Release and deployment** guidance covers pre-release checks, deployment verification, and rollback. **Retrospectives** capture lessons and track improvement actions. The roles-and-personas guide describes the people involved and their responsibilities across these activities.
